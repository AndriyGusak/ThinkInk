ThinkInk listens to your voice and types finished text into any Windows app: no “um”s, correct punctuation, a professional tone or another language — whatever you ask for.

Correction

You speak like a human.
The text comes out like it’s been edited.

  1. Click the microphone in the widget or press your shortcut, and speak into any field: email, messenger, document.
  2. Hesitations disappear. “Um”s, repeats and “no, wait” are removed, and the meaning stays yours.
  3. Finished text appears right where your cursor is. No manual copying.

One thought — eleven ways to put it

Each mode has its own color, and each can have its own shortcut. Pick a mode in the widget, and the same dictated phrase becomes an email, a prompt or a translation.

Correction

Examples show how each mode works. The actual result depends on the model you choose and how you speak.

Select a paragraph. Say what to do with it.

“Make it formal”, “translate into Spanish”, “shorten it but keep all the dates”: in plain words, no commands to memorize. ThinkInk reworks the passage and copies the result to the clipboard. You paste it with Ctrl + V wherever you need it.

The clipboard works in every app, so the result isn’t tied to the window the text came from. Paste it over the selection, into another document, an email or a chat. And you can say the command no matter which window is open.

I won’t get a chance to look at the estimate today. Let’s push the discussion to Friday.I won’t be able to review the estimate today. I suggest we move the discussion to Friday.

Text modes

Choose widget modes and configure their shortcuts.

  • Two themes — Crystal and GraphiteLight, dark or matching the system. Widget and menu glass are set separately.
  • “Result copied”After editing, the text is already on the clipboard, and a small card reminds you: Ctrl + V.
  • History with undoSearch, filter by mode and restore your last ten deletions.
  • Statistics and costsHow many words you dictated in 7, 30 and 365 days, plus a cost calendar for each service.
  • Processing without complicated setupYou choose who recognizes your voice and who works on the text. Anything missing shows up right away, and a short seven-step guide tells you what to do.
  • Tutorial on the real widgetA built-in walkthrough takes you through dictation, translation and editing step by step.

Voice recordings take up no disk space

Audio exists only in memory while it’s being processed, and is never saved as a recording.

History and settings stay in your Windows profile. There’s no separate server holding your texts.

API keys are encrypted by Windows and never shown in the interface.

Recognition can work offline. Whisper runs on your computer, with or without an NVIDIA graphics card. You can choose the model: from the fast Small to the accurate Large v3.

If you choose OpenAI or Gemini for voice, the audio is sent there. For text work (modes, translation, editing), ThinkInk sends text only to the service you chose: OpenAI, Claude or Gemini. The free Gemini key has its own terms: Google may use the texts you send to improve its service.

Good to know up front

  • This is a beta (β) version — Windows x64 only.
  • The app is free for now. A subscription or API key billing is separate, with the service itself.
  • Claude with a subscription works through Claude Code installed on your computer and responds more slowly than with an Anthropic key. Gemini connects only with an API key.
  • Exact OpenAI and Anthropic costs are shown with an admin key. For Gemini, or without such a key, ThinkInk shows an estimate.
  • Live dictation works with Whisper or OpenAI, not with Gemini.
  • Some apps running with elevated rights don’t accept automatic pasting — then you can copy the text from the history.
  • One recording — up to 15 minutes.

5How do I use Gemini for free?

All you need is a free Gemini key. A Google AI subscription won’t work: Google doesn’t allow it in other apps.

  1. Step 1Open Google AI Studio and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Step 2Create a key. Don’t turn on billing.
  3. Step 3Paste the key into ThinkInk under “API keys”.

What to know about free Gemini

  • Google may use your texts, and people may read them.
  • In the EU, the UK and Switzerland, the paid version is required.
  • There’s a daily request limit.
  • Live dictation doesn’t work with Gemini.
